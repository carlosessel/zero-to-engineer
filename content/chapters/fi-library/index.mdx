The most useful free materials behind this course, grouped so you can find the right one quickly. Every one is free, and every link was opened on 23 or 24 November 2026. Rules, rates and limits change, so for anything country-specific always go to the official source listed here rather than to a summary. Pick one resource for the part you are working on, and practise with the projects in <Ref to="fi-projects" />.

## How to use the library

- **Learning something new?** Start with the courses and textbooks in the first section, alongside the matching chapter.
- **Checking a rule, rate or limit?** Go to the official source for your country, and check the date: tax bands, allowances and protection limits change, often in a budget.
- **Checking a firm?** Use the regulator’s own register, never a link the firm sends you (<Ref to="fi-scams" />).
