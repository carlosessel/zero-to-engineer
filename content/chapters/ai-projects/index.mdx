Rules for all of them: public repository, a README with a short demo video, an eval with a reported score, and a paragraph on what failed and how you fixed it. That last paragraph is what interviewers read.

<Note>

Pick problems from your own life and community. A tool that reads local-language voice notes, or answers questions about a school’s handbook, is more memorable than another generic chatbot, and you will know when it is wrong.
