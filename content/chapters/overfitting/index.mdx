## Three splits

- **Training set**: the model learns from this.
- **Validation set**: you use this to choose settings and compare models.
- **Test set**: touched once, at the end, for an honest final score.

## Fixes for overfitting

More data, a simpler model, regularisation (penalising large weights), dropout in neural networks, and stopping training when validation loss stops improving.

## Data leakage

Leakage is when information about the answer sneaks into the inputs: the test rows were used to compute normalisation statistics, the same customer appears in both train and test, or a column was only filled in after the outcome happened. Leaky models score superbly and then fail in production. Split first, then do everything else.
